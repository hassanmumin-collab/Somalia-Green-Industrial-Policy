## 1. Status of each candidate on your list

**National plans and policy frameworks**

- **National Transformation Plan 2025 to 2029.** Exists and confirmed as covering 2025 to 2029 (S-001, full plan; S-002, executive summary dated 23 February 2025). The full plan prints no date.
- **NDP-9 2020 to 2024.** Obtained (S-003).
- **Centennial Vision 2060.** Exists. Published June 2025 (S-004).
- **Industrial policy.** Exists, but only in Somali: the Somali Industrial Development Policy 2021 to 2030, enacted by Prime Minister's Decree No. 128 of 25 February 2021 (S-005). It is a scan and was OCR'd. No English version found.
- **Trade policy.** No standalone trade policy found. The closest documents are the National AfCFTA Implementation Strategy (S-008, September 2023) and the National Export Development and Promotion Strategy (S-007, undated, file created June 2025).
- **Private sector development strategy (Ministry of Commerce and Industry).** Not found. The World Bank and IFC Country Private Sector Diagnostic (S-087) and a 2025 World Bank firm survey (S-088) cover the ground.
- **Investment law.** Three instruments. Foreign Investment Law 2015 (S-017, English). Investment and Investor Protection Law, Law No. 011 signed 20 March 2023 (S-018, Somali). A new Somalia Investment Law passed by Parliament in November 2025, whose text and presidential assent were not found (S-025, not obtained). Which law is in force needs confirming.
- **Special economic zone or industrial park legislation or draft.** None found. The only zone-specific document is UNIDO's September 2022 pre-feasibility study (S-006). One search result reporting a "2024 Investments and Special Economic Zones Bill" concerned Tanzania, not Somalia, and was discarded.

**Climate and environment**

- **Updated NDC (2021).** Obtained (S-020), dated July 2021.
- **Later NDC.** NDC 3.0 exists. Two files are on the UNFCCC site: the June 2025 submission (S-019) and a typeset "official" version posted in September 2025 (S-107). They share the same chapter structure. The figures must be compared in Phase 2, and I have not confirmed which one the NDC registry treats as current.
- **National Adaptation Plan.** No full plan exists. A NAP Framework dated February 2022 exists (S-115), as does the First Adaptation Communication of December 2022 (S-023).
- **National Climate Change Policy.** Obtained (S-022). Cover reads November 2020, updated February 2023.
- **Environmental Protection and Management Act (Law No. 22).** The enacted Somali text is Law No. 22, with the President's signature page dated 13 February 2024 (S-011). The Ministry's English text is dated April 2024 (S-021). The "April 2024 version" in your brief is the English translation; the law itself was signed in February.
- **Law establishing a national environmental management agency.** Not found, as a law or a draft. The 2024 Act creates a National Environment Fund and allows a National Environmental Council, but does not appear to create a separate agency. To confirm in Phase 2.
- **Charcoal, forestry or Prosopis policy.** No federal policy or regulation found. The National Environmental Management Policy (S-120, Somali, February 2023) is the closest instrument and will be checked for charcoal provisions.
- **Clean cooking or household energy strategy.** None found. The closest documents are the Technology Needs Assessment (S-108, S-109) and a UNEP Copenhagen Climate Centre cookstove policy brief (S-110).
- **UN Security Council charcoal ban.** The most recent resolution is 2806 (2025), adopted 12 December 2025 (S-030). Paragraph 14 reaffirms the charcoal import and export ban, with no expiry date. Paragraph 22 renews the maritime interdiction provisions only until 30 November 2026, so a renewal is due within about ten weeks of this inventory.

**Energy**

- **Electricity Act.** Enacted as Law No. 002 of 2023, Somali text only (S-012). No English text of the enacted Act was found. The Ministry's "Electricity Act" download is the 2022 draft bill in Somali, which I did not register.
- **National energy policy or renewable energy strategy.** Not found.
- **Ministry of Energy master plan and off-grid strategy.** The Optimized Cost Electricity Generation and Transmission Development Plan, June 2025 (S-026), and the least-cost electrification study (S-027). No separate off-grid strategy found.

**Productive sectors**

- **Livestock.** Somali Livestock Sector Development Strategy 2020 to 2030 (S-036, dated July 2019). No separate livestock policy found.
- **Agriculture.** National Agricultural Transformation Strategy 2025 to 2029 (S-031); National Irrigation Policy, dated 2019 although posted in 2025 as final (S-032); National Food Security Policy, dated December 2020 and labelled draft by the Ministry (S-034).
- **Fisheries.** The law in force is the Fisheries Development and Management Law, Law No. 008, signed 20 March 2023 (S-013 Somali; S-052 English translation). The 2014 law (S-014) is kept for history.
- **Water.** National Water Resource Strategy 2021 to 2025 (S-028). No federal water law found.
- **Standards.** Somali Standards and Quality Control Law, Law No. 27 of 30 March 2020 (S-015).
- **Halal, veterinary and export health certification.** Animal health law, Law No. 21 of 2016 (S-016). A July 2024 terms of reference for a feasibility study on a unified digital export and animal health certificate system (S-038) indicates that no such system exists yet. No halal certification framework was found, and the Bureau of Standards website could not be captured.

**Statistics and economic data**

- **NBS.**
  - GDP 2025 release at constant 2022 prices (S-040), with the 2024 release (S-051) and methodology (S-041).
  - Inflation Outlook, Policy Brief No. 003, March 2026 (S-042; re-typeset copy S-043).
  - Quarterly Statistical Bulletins for all four quarters of 2024, the latest published (S-044 to S-047).
  - Statistical Abstract 2024 (S-048), Facts and Figures 2024 (S-049), SIHBS 2022 (S-050) and SHDS 2020 (S-059).
  - No separate monthly CPI releases were collected; CPI appears in the bulletins.
- **Central Bank.** Annual Reports 2025, 2024 and 2018 (S-054 to S-056), Quarterly Economic Review 2026 Q1 (S-057) and Annual Statistical Bulletin June 2026 (S-058).
- **IMF.** Latest staff report: fourth ECF review, Country Report 25/335 (S-060). Latest Article IV: 2024, Country Report 24/346 (S-061). No 2026 review found.
- **World Bank.** Somalia Economic Update Edition 11 (2026), titled "Boosting Access to Electricity" (S-062), and Edition 9 (June 2024) on climate change (S-063). Project documents are listed in section 4.
- **UN Comtrade.** Somalia has reported no trade data to UN Comtrade for 2019 to 2024 (checked through the public API). Import values by HS code can only come from partner countries' reported exports to Somalia ("mirror" data). The UAE has no 2024 figure. Extraction of mirror data is deferred to Phase 2 and nothing is stored yet.
- **FAOSTAT.** Production (S-082) and trade (S-083), Somalia rows. FAO publishes no food balance sheets for Somalia.
- **FAO SWALIM.** Site returned errors twice (not obtained). The World Bank Surface Water and Riverine Assessment (S-084) covers the rivers.
- **FSNAU.** February 2022 market update obtained (S-078), plus WFP Market Bulletin June 2022 (S-079). ReliefWeb blocks automated access, so later 2022 updates were not obtained.

**Mogadishu and urban**

- **Banadir Regional Administration plans.** No drainage, sanitation, solid waste or urban development plan found online.
- **World Bank.** The Urban Resilience Project II documents (S-068 appraisal; S-071 March 2026 restructuring; S-072 November 2024 mid-term review) record the citywide drainage design and solid waste mapping for Mogadishu.
- **Land.** The only land instrument found is a 2017 draft bill on urban planning, building and land management (S-070, Somali).

**Model inputs**

- **IMF World Economic Outlook, April 2026 (S-081).** Projections run to 2031. The model will need a stated assumption for 2032.
- **Social accounting matrix or input-output table.** None exists for Somalia. IFPRI data papers for Kenya 2019 (S-085) and Ethiopia 2022 (S-086) were obtained. The matrices themselves are downloadable from Dataverse and CGSpace when the model is built.
- **Plant benchmarks.** See section 5.
- **Solar and storage costs.** AFSIA Africa Solar Outlook 2026 (S-080, Tier 4), ESMAP mini-grid handbook (S-097) and storage report (S-098), and IRENA 2024 costs (S-099).
- **Climate analysis method.** The MCS Playbook (S-119), supplied by you, sets the steps: 1a technical potential, 1b implied cost per ton, 1c caveats, 2a stakeholders, 2b who pays, 3 speed and scale.
- **Emission factors.** IPCC 2006 chapters for stationary combustion, mobile combustion and solid waste (S-102 to S-104). CDM non-renewable biomass default values, which include Somalia (S-100, S-101). CDM methodology drafts AMS-I.L and AMS-I.F (S-105, S-106). The adopted texts of the cookstove, electric vehicle and landfill gas methodologies could not be downloaded (S-118).
- **Diesel generation costs and Mogadishu fuel prices.** To be extracted from S-062, S-067, S-026 and S-079.

**Your research leads.** All were followed to a stored document. The NBS GDP 2025 page leads to S-040; the inflation outlook is S-042; QSB Q1 2024 is S-044; the FAO Hand-in-Hand deck is S-073; the SDRB pages are S-074 to S-077; the RESTORE proposal is S-035; the FSNAU lead is S-078; the Africa Solar Outlook 2026 is S-080. ## 2. Findings that affect the policy

- **Article 6 readiness.** On 3 August 2026 the Ministry of Environment advertised a consultancy to develop Somalia's Article 6 policy framework (S-111). No Carbon Markets bill was found online. Your March 2027 target for the Carbon Markets Act is therefore a live dependency.
- **LT-LEDS.** The Ministry launched a Long-Term Low-Emission Development Strategy on 14 November 2025 (S-112), but the document is not online. It will matter for Part 8 and should be requested.
- **Legal texts are mostly Somali-only scans.** Quotes from them will be in Somali with an English translation in the claim statement. You or a Somali-reading reviewer should confirm those translations, and the fact-checker will compare figures against the page images.
- **Draft or dated documents presented as current.** The irrigation policy "final" file is dated 2019. The food security policy file labelled draft is dated December 2020. The food safety policy is a June 2024 final draft. The urban land law is a 2017 draft. The report must describe these accurately.
- **SDRB web articles carry no citations.** These include the USD 200 million dairy import lead. They are official in origin but will be used only where trade data corroborates them.
