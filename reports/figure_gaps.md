# Figures the report needs that no stored source supports

Prepared for Checkpoint 2 on 23 September 2026; updated for Checkpoint 3 on 24 September 2026 (see the status column and the note at the end). Each gap is a figure or fact named in CLAUDE.md sections 10, 11 or 13 that is not yet backed by a verified claim. In the chapters, each will appear as "I cannot confirm this" with a pending claim until it is sourced or dropped.

The "Suggested route" column says where a figure could come from. It is a lead, not a fact.
