It is my esteemed pleasure to present the 
Somalia Facts and Figures Booklet for the year 
2025. This publication, meticulously compiled 
by the Somalia National Bureau of Statistics 
(SNBS), stands as a cornerstone of official 
statistics, providing essential socio-economic 
data and development indicators for the Federal 
Republic of Somalia.
As 
a 
key 
reference 
for 
policymakers, 
stakeholders, and researchers, this booklet 
offers a comprehensive overview of Somalia’s 
economy, drawing upon a diverse array of data 
sources, including Ministries, Departments, 
and Agencies (MDAs). The information 
presented reflects the nation’s progress and 
trends observed over a five-year period.
It is important to note that while efforts have 
been made to ensure accuracy, certain figures 
may vary from previous editions due to updates. 
Furthermore, the SNBS emphasizes that the 
majority of data presented for 2024/2025 and 
beyond is provisional and subject to revision in 
future publications.
I extend my sincere gratitude to the Federal 
Government institutions and the private sector 
for their invaluable contributions in providing 
the necessary data for this publication. The 
SNBS encourages continued collaboration 
among government agencies to enhance future 
editions of this booklet and other statistical 
publications.
Dr. Abdisalam Abdirahman Mohamed
Director General
Somalia National Bureau of Statistics 
Foreword
The production of this booklet represents 
a significant undertaking by the SNBS, 
aligning with the evolving demands 
of data users in the public and private 
sectors. 
Our 
commitment 
remains 
steadfast in delivering integrated statistical 
information that adheres to national and 
international standards.
I invite users of this publication to share 
their
comments, suggestions, and any additional 
data they deem relevant for future editions. 
Your feedback is of utmost importance 
and can be directed to the contact details 
provided below or accessed through the 
SNBS website: https://nbs.gov.so.
With confidence, I believe that the statistical 
information presented in this booklet will 
empower readers to make well-informed 
decisions. The SNBS, guided by a new 
strategic direction focused on expanded 
statistical coverage through coordination, 
remains dedicated to meeting the data 
requirements of our dynamic and evolving 
nation
